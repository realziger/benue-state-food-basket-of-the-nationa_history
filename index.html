About Benue State

Benue State was created on 3 February 1976 from the former Benue-Plateau State. Its capital is Makurdi, and the state has 23 Local Government Areas.

This starter site is designed for research. Claims about government assets, sales, privatisation or disposal should be supported with official documents and credible reporting before publication.

Peoples, Tribes and Religious Groups

Benue is ethnically diverse. Major groups include the Tiv, Idoma and Igede, alongside several other minority communities and languages.

Christianity is widely practised in the state, while Islam and indigenous/traditional religious practices are also represented. Avoid describing a whole ethnic group as belonging to only one religion.
